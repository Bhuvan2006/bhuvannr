\section*{Why Blog?}

I always had writing blogs and articles on my bucket list. So I thought, if I had a portfolio website, why not add a blog page along with it? So here you are, reading it, friend.

My expectation is to express thoughts clearly. Learning is one thing, but teaching it to others requires words that truly connect. So why not try---and tell you the results after a year?
